SBC NAV Updater — Windows setup

This installer includes the desktop connection, Python and the browser driver.
You do not need Codex or a separate Python installation.

For a new PC:
1. Download the ready-to-use office EXE from MCDO on an already connected office PC.
2. Run that single EXE on the new PC. The private office connection is included.
3. Finish installation and choose "Set up and check this PC".
4. Follow the Windows installation screens for any missing Chrome or IREPS Signer.
5. Connect your token and sign in to IREPS when the updater Chrome window opens.

Token drivers depend on your physical token. Setup detects known token software and provides manufacturer help if its driver is missing. Only a successful IREPS sign-in marks setup complete.

The generic public installer needs a private office connection during onboarding. It cannot authorise an unknown PC by itself.

Then open SBC NAV, choose MCDO and click "Update now". Sign in to IREPS with your digital token when asked. Keep this PC on until the update finishes.

The ready-to-use office installer is private. Share it only with authorised operators. The generic installer contains no connection key, passwords or Google/IREPS sessions.

Uninstalling the app keeps saved progress and connection data in your Windows profile.
